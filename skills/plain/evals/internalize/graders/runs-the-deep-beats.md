The reply is teaching a concept for long-term retention, not giving a quick definition. It PASSES only if ALL of these hold:

1. It builds a sustained ANALOGY drawn from one coherent real-world domain (for example the reader's own field, or everyday life) — not three scattered mini-comparisons.
2. It CONNECTS the concept to something the reader is presumed to already know or hold.
3. It ENDS by posing questions to the reader that it does NOT answer itself — questions demanding the reader explain, apply, or find where the idea breaks.

It FAILS if it is a short one-shot definition, or if it answers its own closing questions, or if it never asks the reader anything.
