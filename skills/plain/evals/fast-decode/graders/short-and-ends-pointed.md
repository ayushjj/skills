The reply explains a technical term to a non-specialist reader. It PASSES only if ALL of these hold:

1. Any jargon used inside the explanation is itself decoded inline — the reader is never left with a second unexplained term.
2. The reply CLOSES with an explicit recommendation, decision, or next step addressed to the reader — for example what to do about it, when they would actually hit it, or what to use instead. A closing sentence that merely restates or summarises the definition FAILS this.
3. It is a compact decode, not a structured lesson: no multi-beat teaching sequence, and it does not end by posing questions for the reader to answer.

Judge only on the three points above.
